🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 26, 2024

What Information We Collect

  • Email Address: Used for account retrieval and dashboard access
  • Display Name: Shown publicly on the leaderboard (your identity)
  • Bracket Name: Your personal label for each bracket (optional)
  • Bracket Predictions: Your team selections and tournament predictions

How We Use Your Information

  • Email: Required to access your dashboard and retrieve your brackets. Never shown publicly.
  • Display Name: Shown on public leaderboards to identify your bracket
  • Display Name Lock: Once you create your first bracket, your display name is locked to ensure consistent identity across all your brackets
  • Bracket Predictions: Stored privately - only visible when you share your tracking link
  • Champion Pick: May be displayed on the leaderboard during the tournament

Accessing Your Data

You can access all your brackets, groups, and data anytime through the Dashboard. Just enter your email address and display name - no password required. Save your tracking link or bookmark your dashboard for easy access.

Newsletter (Optional)

If you check the newsletter box, your email will be added to our World Cup Guide newsletter list. You can unsubscribe at any time. Newsletter signup is completely optional and separate from bracket functionality.

Data Security

Your information is securely stored using Google Workspace enterprise infrastructure. Only authorized personnel have access to this data, and we use industry-standard security measures to protect it.

Your Rights

  • Access your brackets anytime through the dashboard
  • Delete your brackets at any time from your dashboard
  • Request a complete copy of your stored information
  • Opt out of newsletter communications anytime
  • Your email address is never sold or shared with third parties

Data Retention

Bracket data is stored for the duration of the 2026 World Cup tournament and for historical records. You may delete your brackets at any time through your dashboard or contact us for complete data removal.
